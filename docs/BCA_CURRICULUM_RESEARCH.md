**Target institution:** Akash Institute of Management & Science (Akash Group), Bengaluru  
**Programme:** Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA), three years / six semesters  
**Affiliation indicated by the institute:** Bengaluru North University (BNU)

## Read this before implementing

The student is believed to be in the final year, but her admission batch and elective choices have not yet been confirmed. A 2023–2026 student is likely governed by BNU's NEP-2020 curriculum; students admitted from 2024–25 may instead follow SEP-2024. Do not mix the schemes or present either as the student's confirmed curriculum.

Store `scheme`, `academic_year_from`, `academic_year_to`, and `verification_status` with curriculum records. Seed the likely 2023–2026 NEP curriculum as **provisional** until the student supplies a marks card, timetable, syllabus PDF, or UUCMS course list.

Confidence labels used below:

- **Verified-current:** appears in BNU examination material for the relevant 2025–26 final-year cohort.
- **Verified-syllabus:** appears in BNU's V/VI-semester NEP syllabus approved for 2023–24 onward.
- **Curriculum-supported:** appears in BNU/common Karnataka model curriculum or material from a BNU-affiliated college, but exact applicability to Akash is not yet confirmed.
- **Library enrichment:** a useful catalogue recommendation, not a prescribed text.

## Implementation rules for Space Bunny

1. Build scheme-aware Course → Curriculum → Semester → Subject relationships.
2. Permit a Book to map to multiple subjects without duplicating its bibliographic record.
3. Store confidence/source metadata on seeded subject mappings.
4. Present verified and provisional curricula differently in librarian tools; do not expose research uncertainty as confusing student-facing labels.
5. Use the catalogue recommendations to reach a substantial collection, but label mappings as `recommended`, not `prescribed`, unless an official syllabus explicitly lists the book.
6. Do not invent ISBNs, edition years, authors, publishers, course codes, or institute-specific claims.
7. Include core, elective, vocational, skill-enhancement, laboratory, internship, and project classifications.
8. Make elective selection configurable per student/profile; do not assume every student takes every elective.
9. Keep the CSV/import format able to update the scheme after the student supplies official documents.
10. Preserve all existing LibTrack visual work.
